High School Students

Get a head start with Dual Enrollment

Earn transferable college credit while you're still in high school — tuition-free, taught at your school, on our campus, or online.

How to apply

1

Create your account

Set up a free Parchment account to start and track your application.

2

Pick your high school

Select the school you attend to connect to our dual enrollment exchange.

3

Submit your application

Add your info and request parent/guardian consent and high school approval.

4

Register for courses

Once approved, choose your courses and start earning college credit.

Why dual enrollment?

Tuition-free

Eligible high school students pay no enrollment fees for dual enrollment courses.

Real college credit

Earn credit that counts toward your high school diploma and a college degree.

Finish faster

Get ahead on your degree and graduate college sooner.
